Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

Guardians ("the App") is developed by Aleksei (individual developer). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect and process the minimum data necessary for the App to function:

Data Purpose Storage
User ID Unique identifier, auto-generated by the App Device + Supabase
Display name Optional name you choose, shown to your Guardians Device + Supabase
Guardian list User IDs of your designated emergency contacts Device + Supabase
Push notification token Enables delivery of SOS alerts via OneSignal Device + OneSignal

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect or process:

  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Passwords
  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts from your phone
  • Photos, files, or media
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Enable SOS alert delivery to your Guardians
  • Display your name to users who add you as a Guardian
  • Store your guardian list so it persists across sessions

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose other than core App functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — cloud database. Data is stored on Supabase's servers. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • OneSignal (onesignal.com) — push notification service. Only push tokens are shared with OneSignal. See OneSignal Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Locally on your device (using SharedPreferences)
  • In the cloud via Supabase (hosted infrastructure with encryption at rest)

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the App. If you uninstall the App, local data is deleted automatically. To request deletion of your cloud data, contact us at the email below.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access — request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to restriction — request limited processing of your data
  • Right to object — object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [email protected]. We will respond within 30 days.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interest — providing the core functionality of the App (sending SOS alerts to your Guardians)
  • Consent — you voluntarily provide your display name and add Guardians

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the App, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including through our use of Supabase and OneSignal. By using the App, you acknowledge this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
